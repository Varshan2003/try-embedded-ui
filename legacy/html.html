Shortcuts and simulator scope

  

    

      Ctrl/Cmd BCompile
      Ctrl/Cmd EnterRun or pause
      Ctrl/Cmd SSave sketch
      Ctrl/Cmd Alt RReset simulation
      Ctrl/Cmd ZUndo circuit change
      Alt 1–4Switch bottom panel
      Alt BCollapse bottom panel
      RRotate selected part
      DeleteDelete selected part
      Ctrl + wheelZoom canvas
    

    
Wiring: click a component terminal, then click a board pin. Drag the canvas background to pan.

    
The interpreter covers the Arduino core: pin, timing, PWM, interrupt, tone and Serial functions, plus the Servo class. Pointer arithmetic, classes, structs, and third-party libraries beyond Servo are not implemented, and I2C/SPI calls are logged to the event timeline rather than simulated at the bus level.
